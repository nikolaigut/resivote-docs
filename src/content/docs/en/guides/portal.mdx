ResiVote's **Owner Portal** (`/portal`) is the primary digital hub for co-owners (STWEG/MEG). It brings together daily property operations—from reviewing owned units and value quotas to quick access for upcoming meetings, announcements, service tickets, and the community forum.

<Aside type="note" title="Role Responsibilities in the Portal">
- **`eigentümer` (Co-owner):** Primarily uses the portal on **tablet or smartphone (mobile)** for daily overview, reviewing unit quotas, participating in meetings, and contacting property management.
- **`tenant_admin` (Management):** Manages communities, units, owner accounts, and published documents on **desktop & tablet**.
</Aside>

## 2. Next Meeting Quick Access

Whenever a general meeting is approaching or currently live, the dashboard highlights it prominently:

- **Live Status ("Active"):** When a meeting is in *Check-In* or *In Progress*, a green live indicator appears alongside a **"Join Now"** button leading directly into the meeting room or hall voting screen.
- **Agenda Preview:** Clicking **"View Agenda"** opens a modal with all agenda items, draft motions, and linked attachments (e.g., quotes, plans, financial statements).
- **Calendar Export:** The **"Export Calendar"** button downloads an `.ics` file that adds the meeting directly to Apple Calendar, Google Calendar, or Outlook.
- **Location:** Displays whether the meeting takes place in person (with physical address) or online / hybrid.

## 3. My Units and Value Quotas (MEA)

Under **"My Units"**, all apartments, parking spaces, or storage rooms belonging to the signed-in owner are listed.

### Value Quota / Co-ownership Share (MEA)
Displays the registered land registry value quota (e.g., `45 / 1000`). If an owner holds multiple units, the portal automatically aggregates the total quota.

### Primary Voter Badge
If a unit is owned jointly (e.g., by married couples or heir communities), the designated voting delegate under Swiss law (Art. 712l SCC) is marked with the **"Primary Voter"** badge.

### Built-in STWEG Glossary
An info button next to the quotas opens the **STWEG Glossary**, explaining Swiss condominium law (Art. 712a–712t SCC) in simple terms:
- **Value quota (thousandths):** Key for cost allocation and qualified majority calculations.
- **Head count:** One vote per owner, regardless of the number of units owned.
- **Simple majority:** Majority of votes present or represented.
- **Qualified majority:** Majority of all co-owners by head count who also hold more than half of all value quotas.
- **Unanimity:** Approval of all co-owners without exception (e.g. for luxury alterations under Art. 647e SCC).
