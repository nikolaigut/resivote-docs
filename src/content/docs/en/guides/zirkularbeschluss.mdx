Not every co-ownership decision requires convening an in-person or hybrid general meeting. For routine approvals, urgent interim authorizations, or time-shifted votes, ResiVote supports **circular resolutions** (Art. 712m para. 2 Swiss Civil Code / ZGB) fully digitally in the Owner Portal.

<Aside type="note" title="Role Responsibilities in Circular Resolutions">
- **`tenant_admin` (Management):** Prepares the circular resolution on **desktop or tablet**, formulates motions, attaches supporting files, and sets the voting deadline.
- **`eigentümer` (Co-owner):** Reviews the resolution package on **tablet or mobile**, casts an online vote (Yes / No / Abstain), and inspects the final tally.
</Aside>

---

## 1. Legal Foundations in Switzerland

Under Swiss condominium law (**Art. 712m para. 2 SCC**), decisions by circular letter generally require the **consent of all co-owners** (unanimity), unless the community's bylaws expressly permit a qualified or simple majority for written ballots.

ResiVote securely accommodates both models:
- **Unanimity Mode:** A motion only passes if every single eligible co-owner votes "Yes".
- **Statutory Quorum:** Evaluates votes against the registered majority requirements when the deadline elapses.

<Steps>
  1. **Title & Deadline:** Set clear start and end dates for voting (e.g. 20-day return window).
  2. **Formulate Motions:** Draft precise proposal wording.
  3. **Attach Documents:** Include audit reports, architectural plans, or contractor quotes as PDFs.
  4. **Publish:** Co-owners receive automated email and push alerts and gain instant voting access in the portal.
</Steps>
