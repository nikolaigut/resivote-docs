ResiVote replaces paper pinboards in building hallways with a centralized, **digital announcement system**. Property managers publish notices promptly, and co-owners stay informed anywhere.

<Aside type="note" title="Role Responsibilities in Announcements">
- **`tenant_admin` (Management):** Drafts notices on **desktop**, chooses priority (normal or urgent), defines audience communities, and publishes circulars.
- **`eigentümer` (Co-owner):** Reads notices on **tablet or mobile**, marks messages as read, or moves them to the archive.
</Aside>

---

## 1. Management Notice Administration

**Responsible Role: `tenant_admin`**  
*Device: Desktop*

In the admin area under **"Announcements"** (`/admin/messages`), property management oversees all notices:

![Announcements in Admin View](/screenshots/tenant-admin/desktop/admin-messages.png)
*Desktop view for `tenant_admin`: Comprehensive overview of all circulars, release dates, and recipient communities.*

### Priority Levels
1. **Normal:** Routine notices such as garden landscaping schedules, meter reading dates, or general building information.
2. **Urgent / Important:** Time-sensitive disruptions such as sudden elevator outages, heating failures, water shutoffs, or garage ramp closures. Urgent notices are visually highlighted.

## 3. Co-owner Capabilities

<Steps>
  1. **Unread Indicator:** New announcements are color-coded with an "Unread" badge.
  2. **Mark as Read:** Click **"Mark as read"** or use the bulk **"Mark all as read"** action.
  3. **Archiving:** Handled notices can be archived into the **"Archived"** tab to keep the active feed clean.
  4. **Full-Text Search:** Quickly search through past notices using keywords (e.g., "elevator", "parking", "heating").
</Steps>
