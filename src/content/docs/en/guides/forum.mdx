ResiVote's **Community Forum** offers co-owners a private, dedicated space for neighborhood dialog. Whether discussing draft agenda items before a meeting, reviewing house rules, or planning joint initiatives like rooftop solar panels, the forum fosters alignment early.

<Aside type="note" title="Role Responsibilities in the Forum">
- **`eigentümer` (Co-owner):** Starts discussions, engages with neighbors on **tablet or smartphone**, comments on threads, and shares ideas.
- **`tenant_admin` (Management):** Moderates topics on **desktop**, pins official notices, and locks concluded threads.
</Aside>

---

## 1. Forum Moderation by Management

**Responsible Role: `tenant_admin`**  
*Device: Desktop*

In the admin area under **"Forum"** (`/admin/forum`), administrators oversee discussions across all managed properties:

![Forum Moderation in Admin](/screenshots/tenant-admin/desktop/admin-forum.png)
*Desktop view for `tenant_admin`: Moderation dashboard, managing topics, and locking concluded discussions.*

### Moderation Tools
- **Lock / Close Topics:** Prevent further replies once a matter has been resolved or delegated to an official meeting.
- **Enforce Netiquette:** Remove inappropriate or off-topic posts.
- **Community Filtering:** Filter topics by specific condominium association.
