ResiVote's integrated **ticketing and inquiry system** replaces disjointed emails and calls with a structured, trackable reporting process. Co-owners submit maintenance requests and administrative inquiries directly from the portal—including on-site photos captured with their smartphone camera.

<Aside type="note" title="Role Responsibilities in Tickets">
- **`eigentümer` (Co-owner):** Submits inquiries and defect reports on **tablet or mobile**, uploads photos, and replies in the message thread.
- **`tenant_admin` (Management):** Processes tickets on **desktop**, assigns priorities and staff, converses with owners, and converts items into meeting agenda topics.
</Aside>

---

## 1. Ticket Management by Property Administrators

**Responsible Role: `tenant_admin`**  
*Device: Desktop*

In the admin area under **"Tickets"** (`/admin/tickets`), management reviews all reported issues across managed properties:

![Ticket Management in Admin](/screenshots/tenant-admin/desktop/admin-tickets.png)
*Desktop view for `tenant_admin`: Central ticket queue with filters for community, status, and category.*

### Ticket Status Workflow
- **Open:** Newly submitted issue awaiting triage.
- **In Progress:** Service technician dispatched or clarifications under way.
- **Resolved:** Required maintenance completed.
- **Closed:** Formally closed by owner or administration.

Administrators can convert tickets into actionable tasks or formal agenda items for the next co-owners' meeting with one click.

## 2. Submitting Tickets as a Co-owner

**Perspective of Role: `eigentümer`**  
*Devices: Tablet or Smartphone (Mobile)*

Co-owners manage their requests under **"Tickets"** (`/portal/tickets`).

### Creating a New Ticket on Mobile

Submitting a defect is optimized for mobile on-site capture:

![Creating a Ticket on Smartphone](/screenshots/owner/mobile/portal-ticket-create-mobile.png)
*Mobile view for `eigentümer`: Modal dialog with property selection, subject, category, description, and direct camera upload.*

<Steps>
  1. **Select Community:** If the owner has units in multiple buildings.
  2. **Category & Subject:** Categorize under *Damage / Maintenance*, *Billing / Financials*, or *General / Motion*.
  3. **Description:** Describe the problem precisely (e.g., "Underground garage barrier does not open via remote").
  4. **Attach Photo:** Snap a picture on site and attach it instantly.
  5. **Submit:** Property management receives an immediate notification.
</Steps>
