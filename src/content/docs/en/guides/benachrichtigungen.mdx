To ensure co-owners stay informed without feeling overwhelmed, ResiVote provides a **granular notification matrix**. Each owner chooses exactly which channels and topics trigger alerts.

<Aside type="note" title="Role Responsibilities in Notifications">
- **`eigentümer` (Co-owner):** Customizes preferences in the portal on **tablet or mobile** across all life areas.
- **`tenant_admin` (Management):** Triggers system notices automatically when meetings are published or decisions logged.
</Aside>

## 3. The 5 Notification Categories

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Meetings" icon="calendar">
    Invitations to ordinary and extraordinary meetings, pre-meeting reminders, check-in opening, and published minutes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Documents" icon="document">
    Uploads of new bylaws, annual accounts, audit reports, or templates by property management.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Community Forum" icon="comment">
    New discussion topics or replies to your comments in the owner forum.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Service Tickets" icon="warning">
    Status changes and replies from management regarding your maintenance tickets.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Announcements" icon="email">
    Official circulars and urgent building notices (e.g., maintenance schedules).
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

---

## 4. Channels

- **Email Notifications:** Formal notices with direct links to documents, tickets, or meetings.
- **Push Notifications (Browser & Mobile):** Instant alerts on smartphone or tablet for urgent matters.
- **In-App Bell:** Top-header notification drawer displaying recent activity with direct jump links.
